JCR/JIF, JCR/AIS, SJR, CiteScore percentiles, and unrelated journal-ranking services
are not interchangeable. A journal can have different quartiles by category/year.
Indexing alone is not evidence of Q1 standing. Do not cite a 2025 secondary report as
confirmed current 2026 JCR data.

## Fees and reproducibility

Elsevier's publisher pricing policy distinguishes subscription-funded articles from
author-funded open access: https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/pricing.
That policy does not establish the payable charge or a fee-free route for any specific
submission. No fee figure is asserted because accessible secondary prices conflict
and current journal-specific author terms were not directly verified.

Before submission, verify the chosen journal's current guide, article type, anonymity,
AI-use disclosures, authorship rules, ethics/data statements, code/data deposit policy,
and all mandatory or optional charges. Meanwhile implement source manifests, executable
experiments, environment locks, fixed seeds, held-out temporal evaluation, and a claim
audit. These are our research standards, not a fabricated journal mandate.

## Decision

Keep all three on a conditional shortlist. Choose after the pilot clarifies whether
the strongest supported contribution is technical evaluation, decision support, or
financial interpretation. Primary current Q1 verification remains an explicit access
limitation; it is not resolved by repeating an aggregator's label.
