# Evaluation protocol revision: decision required before analysis freeze

Reviewed 27 October 2026 against the preserved official-source snapshot. Status:
**approved by direct user reply; see protocol_decision.md**. At comparison time
no model was fitted, no preprocessing was
learned and no final-test performance was inspected to choose these alternatives.

The original 2019--2021 validation block has no positive failures. Its 6,621
financially usable, observable, unpurged rows in the Step 7 report cannot support
failure discrimination or ordinary failure-based model selection. The count
comparison below uses usable/observable rows before legacy split purging; each
proposed model instead enforces its own strict historical maturity cutoff.

Candidate counts and exact annual fold origins are generated by
`uv run --locked risktrace review-protocol` into results/protocol_review.
Independent events here mean distinct failing CERTs, not statistically independent
economic shocks. Related failures and common time shocks can further reduce
effective information. Do not add per-year bank counts to obtain pooled events.

## Recommended Option A, if approved

For each year 2012 through 2020, fit at the first report's assumed availability
date (31 March + 60 days = 30 May). Evaluate all four report quarters of that year.
Expand training only to records whose **entire 12-month outcome horizon ends
strictly before that model origin**, for both label classes. Do not admit a positive
early merely because its eventual failure is already known. The first fold trains
on 2010 (2,608 usable rows, 16 positive banks); the last trains through 2018.

Pooled out-of-fold validation contains 17 positive quarters from six banks. Keep
all zero-event years. Estimate prespecified pooled development metrics; show annual
counts and undefined annual discrimination metrics transparently. Overlapping
quarters for the same failure can appear in adjacent validation years. Bank/time
dependence and low event count must constrain comparisons, calibration and CIs.
This does not establish adequate power for small architecture improvements.

Hyperparameters, thresholds and calibration rules must be selected before the first
2022 forecast origin. All candidate 2012--2020 validation outcomes mature before
30 May 2022. **Do not use the entire 2021 validation/OOF outcome set**: later 2021
reports' labels mature during 2023, after some test forecasts. Using them for early
test model selection/calibration would leak future outcome information even if
their report dates are earlier than 2022.

Recommended final fit: static weights trained as of 30 May 2022, using complete
outcome windows strictly before that date (latest eligible report: 2020Q4;
31,319 usable labeled rows and 22 distinct positive banks). Forecast all original
2022--2025 test quarters without selecting settings on them. 2021 features may
warm temporal states only as they become available, not fit preprocessing or
provide unavailable labels. Any rolling test-period refitting requires a separate,
prespecified deployment protocol, not an unannounced reuse of test labels.

The four positive test banks are already known from the earlier coverage audit.
The holdout is performance-unexamined, not completely unseen. Keep its original
membership; do not optimize dates or models against those known cases.

## Earlier-history feasibility, not adoption

Three official-source probes are preserved in
data/manifests/protocol_probe_sources.json. The API returned 523 eligible financial
records for 2000Q1 and 685 for 2009Q1. One sampled report at each date contained
the 22 requested field names. The 2000--2009 failure list returned 210 records
across institution sizes/types. **These are not 210 eligible additional bank
failures**: no full historical panel/intersection has been built or validated.
Coverage/semantics across the decade, legal-entity/lifecycle consistency, availability
and the incremental independent event budget remain unverified.

An extension could supply more training/validation events but cannot manufacture
additional failures in the fixed final holdout. It changes the study period and
requires author approval, source validation and a new dataset version. Do not
download/adopt a complete extended analysis cohort or broaden labels silently.

## Decision and stop conditions

Recommend A for bounded exploratory research while retaining the original bank
failure outcome, asset band, study period and final holdout. Six positive validation
banks are a severe limitation; neither A nor B makes the project Q1-ready. If a
strong predictive-improvement claim is essential, assess C first and revisit power
before costly neural training. A methods/feasibility-only outcome is also honest.

The user selected Option A after the concrete comparison. Proceed with its
forecast-clock contracts and freeze, retaining the six-bank power limitation.
The recorded choice authorizes this bounded exploratory analysis; it does not
establish Q1-level predictive evidence or approve the unadopted history extension.
